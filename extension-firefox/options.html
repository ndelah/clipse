Privacy & API costsWhat stays here, and what goes to AI.

Clipse is free, with no Clipse subscription. You pay your AI provider directly for what you use.

Your keys and history stay in this Chrome profile. Keys are excluded from history backups. Saving to Notion sends that summary to Notion.

Captions and video metadata go directly to OpenAI for processing. With Jev selected, excerpts and metadata also go to TypeSafe. AI processing happens at these providers.

Summary requests use your API credit. Longer videos may need several paid requests. A connection check verifies key and model access, but cannot check your available credit.
