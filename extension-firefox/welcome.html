    Get set up

    Ready in a few minutes

    1. 1

      Create an OpenAI API key

      Clipse is free; AI processing is billed by OpenAI to your own account. A ChatGPT subscription doesn’t include API usage. Get a key ↗

    2. 2

      Paste it into Settings

      Clipse checks the key and model access before saving.

    3. 3

      Choose where your notes go

      Clipse shows only the buttons you pick. Change it any time in Settings.

      Where will your notes go?

      Connecting Notion (free, about a minute). Open Settings, choose Notion, and follow these steps there:

      1. Create an internal integration ↗ and copy its secret.
      2. Open the Notion page for your summaries, choose ••• → Connections, and add the integration. Help ↗
      3. Paste the secret and the page link into Clipse’s Settings.

    4. 4

      Pin Clipse and open a video

      Pin it from Chrome’s puzzle-piece menu so your library is one click away.
